Load it in Power BI

In Power BI Desktop choose Get data → Excel, select the cleaned workbook, choose the sheet, and select Load. For a CSV or JSON raw file, choose its matching connector.

03

Build your report

Drag fields into the report canvas, add slicers for dates or assets, and choose charts such as line, column, cards, maps, or tables. Save the finished report as a .pbix file.

▦

Why use the cleaned Excel export?

The cleaned export is always delivered as .xlsx, even when the original upload was CSV, XLS, or JSON. This gives Power BI one predictable input format while preserving the raw download separately.
